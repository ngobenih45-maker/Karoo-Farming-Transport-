01 / WHO WE ARE

Transport built around trust and delivery.

Karoo Farming Transport is a South African transport and logistics company operating from Mieliefontein Farm, off the N1 Highway near Hanover.

We support customers who need goods moved with care and clear communication. Whether the load is agricultural produce, retail stock, furniture, construction material or heavy cargo, our focus stays the same: a practical transport solution and a dependable service from collection to delivery.
