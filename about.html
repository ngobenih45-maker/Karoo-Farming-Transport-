OUR STORY

A transport partner
you can count on.

Karoo Farming Transport is a South African transport and logistics business based at Off N1 Highway, Mieliefontein Farm, Hanover.

We provide transport solutions for businesses, agricultural operations and individuals moving goods across South Africa. Our services cover general cargo, furniture, building materials, food and perishables, agricultural products, machinery and heavy or bulk loads.

Our approach is built on practical service values: handle loads responsibly, communicate clearly and respect delivery commitments. We work to match each customer's needs with a suitable transport option.
